OPRivView Replay

Needs macOS 14 or later, on {{ARCH_NEEDED}}.

To install
1. Drag OPRivView Replay onto the Applications folder.
2. Eject this disk.

The first time you open it
This app is not notarized by Apple, so macOS will refuse it the first time and say it cannot check it.
1. Open the app from Applications and click Done (not Move to Trash).
2. Open System Settings, then Privacy & Security, and scroll down to Security.
3. Click Open Anyway beside OPRivView Replay, and confirm with your password or Touch ID.
You only need to do this once.

Using it
Open the app's settings to find your comma on the network, or to add a comma Connect token. Public drives need neither:
paste the drive's address into the app.

Licences for the software inside are in the app: Contents/Resources/licenses.
