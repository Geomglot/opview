OPRivView Replay

Needs 64-bit Windows 10 or 11.

To install
1. Unzip this folder somewhere with a short path, for example into Documents, Downloads or onto the Desktop. Nothing
   else is installed. (Windows cannot open files more than 260 characters deep, so a folder buried many levels down
   may fail to unzip.)
2. Open the folder and double-click OPRivViewReplay.exe.
To remove it, delete the folder. Your settings are in %APPDATA%\OPRivViewReplay and downloaded drives in
%LOCALAPPDATA%\OPRivViewReplay; delete those too if you want them gone.

The first time you open it
This program is not signed, so Windows SmartScreen may say it protected your PC.
Click "More info", then "Run anyway". You only need to do this once.

Using it
Open the app's settings to find your comma on the network, or to add a comma Connect token. Public drives need neither:
paste the drive's address into the app.
Reaching the comma itself uses Windows' own OpenSSH, which Windows 10 and 11 normally have. If the app says ssh was not
found, add "OpenSSH Client" in Settings > Apps > Optional features.
Finished clips are saved in your Videos folder unless you choose another.

Licences for the software inside are in the licenses folder.
